# Brand Intelligence Research  
**Measuring Brand Visibility in Large Language Models (LLMs)**

This repository curates **research papers, methodologies, and evaluation frameworks** focused on measuring and understanding **brand visibility, attribution, and prominence in Large Language Models (LLMs)** and generative search systems.

The primary motivation is to support research and applied work in:
- Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
- Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)
- LLM auditing and evaluation
- Brand intelligence in AI-driven discovery systems

---

## 📌 Scope

The repository focuses on:
- How and when brands are mentioned by LLMs
- Attribution, grounding, and citation behavior
- Visibility metrics beyond traditional SEO
- Prompt-based auditing and evaluation methodologies
- Black-box analysis of generative systems
